    How it works
    1. 1 · Split The text is cleaned and split into sentences with NLTK. Fragments without a verb (headings, addresses) are dropped.
    2. 2 · Classify Legal-BERT, a BERT model pretrained on legal text and fine-tuned on 9,400 labeled ToS sentences, scores every sentence as fair, potentially unfair, or clearly unfair.
    3. 3 · Rewrite Only the flagged clauses go to BART, fine-tuned on plain-English rewrites written by Claude (knowledge distillation). The most severe clauses go first.

    Everything runs on a Raspberry Pi 5 with no GPU. Sorting sentences is quick; rewriting takes about 6 seconds per flagged clause, which is why the summaries trickle in.
